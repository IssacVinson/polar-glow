Polar Glow Detailing Account Deletion

App Name: Polar Glow Detailing

Developer: Issac Vinson

Delete your account in the app (required by Apple)

  1. Open Polar Glow Detailing and sign in
  2. Go to Account (or Settings)
  3. Tap Delete account
  4. Enter your password to confirm

This permanently deletes your Firebase login, profile, reviews, and personal booking details. Completed paid jobs may be kept in anonymized form for business records.

Email fallback

If you cannot use the in-app flow, email polarglowdetailing@gmail.com with subject Account Deletion Request and the email address you used to sign up. We will process the request within 7 business days.

Note: Account deletion cannot be undone.
