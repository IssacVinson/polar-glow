How to delete your account

To request deletion of your Polar Glow Detailing account and all associated data (including bookings, profile, and any stored information):

  1. Email us at: polarglowdetailing@gmail.com
  2. Use subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the email address you used to sign up in the Polar Glow app

We will confirm receipt and process your request within 7 business days.

Note: This will permanently delete your account and all data. This action cannot be undone.
